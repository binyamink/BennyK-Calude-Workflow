# Writing Style Conventions

## Titles & Headings

- **No periods** at the end of section/subsection titles
- Periods are OK on paragraph-level titles (e.g., `\paragraph{Proof sketch.}`)
- Sentence case for slide titles: "Trade costs and gravity" not "Trade Costs and Gravity"

## Prose

- Plain language — avoid unnecessary jargon
- Active voice where possible
- Define technical terms on first use
- Short sentences in slide content; longer explanations in notes/papers
